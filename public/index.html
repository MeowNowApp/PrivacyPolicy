MeowNow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2025

Overview

MeowNow is a cat image sharing app that allows users to view, upload, and vote on cat images. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Images: Cat photos and GIFs you choose to upload
  • Votes: Your likes/dislikes on images
  • Device Information: iOS device type and version for compatibility

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App interactions for improving user experience
  • Technical Data: Crash logs and performance metrics through iOS analytics
  • Anonymous Analytics: App usage patterns (no personal identification)

AI Breed Detection (Optional Feature)

  • Image Analysis: When enabled, cat images are analyzed locally on your device using Apple Intelligence
  • No Cloud Processing: Breed detection happens entirely on-device; images are not sent to external servers for AI analysis
  • Entertainment Only: AI breed identification is for entertainment purposes and may not be accurate

How We Use Your Information

  • Image Sharing: Display your uploaded images to other users
  • Leaderboards: Show anonymous upload statistics and voting results
  • AI Breed Detection: Analyze cat images locally on your device to identify breeds (opt-in feature)
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage to enhance features and performance
  • Technical Support: Debug issues and improve app stability

Data Storage and Security

  • Cloud Storage: Images stored securely on AWS S3 servers
  • Encryption: All data transmitted using industry-standard encryption
  • Local Storage: Temporary image caching on your device for performance
  • On-Device AI: Breed detection processing happens entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence
  • No Personal Data: We don't collect names, emails, or contact information

Data Sharing

  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers
  • Public Images: Uploaded images are visible to all app users
  • Anonymous Statistics: Only aggregate, non-identifying usage data may be shared

Your Rights

  • Image Control: You own the rights to images you upload
  • Data Access: Contact us to request information about your data
  • Deletion: Remove your images through the app's delete functionality

Age Requirements

MeowNow is suitable for all ages. No personal information is collected from users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Significant changes will be announced through app updates.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, please contact us through the app's feedback feature.

This privacy policy complies with iOS App Store requirements and applicable privacy regulations.
